**结论**

- `backend/app/ai/context/engine.py` 现在需要 `_build_web_research_continuation_context`，因为 `ConversationContextEngine.assemble()` 新增了“先做 provisional capability bundle，再做 intent planning”的流程：先在 [backend/app/ai/context/engine.py:285](E:/git_clone/novusai-saas-yudi/backend/app/ai/context/engine.py#L285) 调 `self.base_engine._build_web_research_continuation_context(...)`，再在 [backend/app/ai/context/engine.py:293](E:/git_clone/novusai-saas-yudi/backend/app/ai/context/engine.py#L293) 和 [backend/app/ai/context/engine.py:297](E:/git_clone/novusai-saas-yudi/backend/app/ai/context/engine.py#L297) 把它传给 `IntentPlanner.plan_turn(...)`。`IntentPlanner` 会在 [backend/app/ai/engine/intent_planner.py:679](E:/git_clone/novusai-saas-yudi/backend/app/ai/engine/intent_planner.py#L679) 到 [backend/app/ai/engine/intent_planner.py:689](E:/git_clone/novusai-saas-yudi/backend/app/ai/engine/intent_planner.py#L689) 用这个 continuation context 把“那个链接/上一个结果”这类续问继续判成 `web_research`。随后 `engine.py` 又在 [backend/app/ai/context/engine.py:359](E:/git_clone/novusai-saas-yudi/backend/app/ai/context/engine.py#L359) 和 [backend/app/ai/context/engine.py:364](E:/git_clone/novusai-saas-yudi/backend/app/ai/context/engine.py#L364) 依据 `has_web_research_intent` 决定是否注入 web research date anchor；而 date anchor 本身也依赖“上轮是否刚做过 web_search/fetch_url + 本轮是否 generic follow-up”的判断，见 [backend/app/ai/context/engine.py:1019](E:/git_clone/novusai-saas-yudi/backend/app/ai/context/engine.py#L1019) 到 [backend/app/ai/context/engine.py:1029](E:/git_clone/novusai-saas-yudi/backend/app/ai/context/engine.py#L1029)。本质上，这是把 `BaseEngine._prepare_execution()` 里原本已有的 continuation 逻辑在 context 组装阶段前移复用，原实现见 [backend/app/ai/engine/base.py:2519](E:/git_clone/novusai-saas-yudi/backend/app/ai/engine/base.py#L2519)；helper 定义在 [backend/app/ai/engine/base.py:2390](E:/git_clone/novusai-saas-yudi/backend/app/ai/engine/base.py#L2390)。

- 除了你点名的 `_BaseEngineStub`，当前仓库里没有第二个“已经被传给 `ConversationContextEngine/get_context_engine` 的 stub/fake/mock”缺这个方法。`get_context_engine()` 的契约就是 `base_engine: BaseEngine`，见 [backend/app/ai/context/engine.py:1163](E:/git_clone/novusai-saas-yudi/backend/app/ai/context/engine.py#L1163) 和 [backend/app/ai/context/engine.py:1166](E:/git_clone/novusai-saas-yudi/backend/app/ai/context/engine.py#L1166)。测试里唯一 fake 传入点是 `_BaseEngineStub`，见 [backend/tests/integration/ai/test_context_engine_capabilities.py:85](E:/git_clone/novusai-saas-yudi/backend/tests/integration/ai/test_context_engine_capabilities.py#L85) 和 [backend/tests/integration/ai/test_context_engine_capabilities.py:87](E:/git_clone/novusai-saas-yudi/backend/tests/integration/ai/test_context_engine_capabilities.py#L87)；它现在也已经补了该方法，见 [backend/tests/integration/ai/test_context_engine_capabilities.py:43](E:/git_clone/novusai-saas-yudi/backend/tests/integration/ai/test_context_engine_capabilities.py#L43)。另一处传入的是实际 `ConversationEngine`，不是 stub，见 [backend/tests/services/test_conversation_engine_prepare_execution.py:1406](E:/git_clone/novusai-saas-yudi/backend/tests/services/test_conversation_engine_prepare_execution.py#L1406) 和 [backend/tests/services/test_conversation_engine_prepare_execution.py:1422](E:/git_clone/novusai-saas-yudi/backend/tests/services/test_conversation_engine_prepare_execution.py#L1422)。

- 仅有一个“潜在后续风险”值得记：`backend/tests/services/test_stream_handler_real_stream.py` 的 `_FakeEngine` 没有这个方法，类定义起点在 [backend/tests/services/test_stream_handler_real_stream.py:93](E:/git_clone/novusai-saas-yudi/backend/tests/services/test_stream_handler_real_stream.py#L93)，其方法列表到 [backend/tests/services/test_stream_handler_real_stream.py:305](E:/git_clone/novusai-saas-yudi/backend/tests/services/test_stream_handler_real_stream.py#L305) 为止也没有 `_build_web_research_continuation_context`，子类 `_BrokenStreamEngine` / `_BlockingThinkingStreamEngine` 见 [backend/tests/services/test_stream_handler_real_stream.py:311](E:/git_clone/novusai-saas-yudi/backend/tests/services/test_stream_handler_real_stream.py#L311) 和 [backend/tests/services/test_stream_handler_real_stream.py:318](E:/git_clone/novusai-saas-yudi/backend/tests/services/test_stream_handler_real_stream.py#L318)。但它们当前只喂给 `StreamExecutionHandler`，不走 `ConversationContextEngine` 契约，所以现在不会因这个方法报错；只有以后把这套 fake 拿去跑 `_prepare_execution` / `get_context_engine()` 才会出问题。
